# Venue support and admissions

The venue sells tickets to events and runs a support desk.

## Admissions

1. A visitor can buy a ticket for an event; the ticket records the event, the seat, the price paid and the buyer's email.
2. A ticket is issued when paid and is scanned at the gate; a scanned ticket must never be scanned again.
3. A visitor can transfer an unscanned ticket to another visitor.
4. Tickets are not refundable once the event has started.

## Support desk

5. A visitor can open a ticket with the support desk describing a problem; the ticket records the visitor, the subject, the description and a severity.
6. A support agent can assign a ticket to themselves, reply to it, and close it; a closed ticket cannot be reopened by the visitor after 14 days.
7. A support ticket must not be closed without at least one reply from an agent.
8. The support desk manager sees open tickets per agent.

## Constraints

- Team of 4, Java, PostgreSQL available.
