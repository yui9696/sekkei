# 🧪 TrialBridge — eConsent & randomisation for multi-site trials

> 💡 **Callout:** This page is the source of truth for the Q1 build. Comments go in the thread, not inline.

👤 Owner: Mira Osei (Clinical Ops) · 🏷️ Tags: clinical, GxP, eConsent · 📅 Updated Sep 12

## 🎯 What we are building

A web application that lets participants at up to 60 investigator sites read and sign informed-consent forms electronically, and that randomises enrolled participants to a study arm according to the protocol's allocation scheme.

## 🧩 Requirements

▶ **Consent**
- Participants read the consent form on a tablet at the site and sign with a drawn signature; the site investigator counter-signs.
- Every version of a consent form is kept; a participant must re-consent when the form changes, and the system records which version they signed.
- A signed consent is a PDF with an audit trail page (who, when, IP, form version) — 21 CFR Part 11 applies.
- Participants can withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal stops further data collection for that participant within 24 hours.

▶ **Randomisation**
- Study coordinators randomise a participant after eligibility is confirmed; allocation uses permuted blocks stratified by site and by age group, with block sizes defined in the protocol.
- The allocation must be concealed: nobody at the site can see the next assignment, and the sequence is generated by the unblinded statistician's upload.
- Emergency unblinding: an investigator can unblind one participant with a reason; the sponsor's medical monitor is notified immediately.
- Drug kit assignment follows randomisation; when a site has fewer than 3 kits of an arm in stock, the depot is notified to resupply.

▶ **Reporting**
- Sponsors see enrolment per site per week and screen-failure reasons.
- The full audit trail is exportable for an FDA inspection within one business day of the request.

<details>
<summary>📐 Numbers</summary>

- 60 sites, 2,400 participants over 18 months, roughly 20 randomisations a day at peak.
- Consent PDFs: about 40 pages each with the audit page.
- Audit trail retained for 25 years after study close (sponsor policy).

</details>

<details>
<summary>⚠️ Things that must never happen</summary>

- The same participant must never be randomised twice.
- A site user must never see allocations they did not make.
- Audit records must never be edited or deleted, including by administrators.

</details>

## 🛠️ Tech and team

> ⚙️ We are a Python shop: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React. Hosted on Azure (EU regions only, GDPR). SSO through the sponsor's Azure AD for staff; participants do not have accounts. Team: 4 engineers, 1 QA, 1 validation lead.

> 🚫 **Not in scope:** EDC (case report forms) — that stays in Medidata Rave. Drug accountability beyond kit counts.
