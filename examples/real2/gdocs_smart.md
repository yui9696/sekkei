Customer Feedback Portal

Requirements
• Customers must be able to submit feedback with a “category” and an optional screenshot (max 10 MB).
• Support agents must see new feedback within 30 seconds.
• The portal must handle 5,000 submissions/day and 50 requests/s at peak.
• Feedback must be deleted after 2 years.

Constraints
• Team of 4 – TypeScript on Node.js – PostgreSQL available.
• Customers log in with the company’s existing OIDC provider.
