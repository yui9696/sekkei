## Non-functional requirements
- 2,000 concurrent users at peak; 300 requests/s.
- Availability 99.9 %.
- Patron data retained for 7 years after account closure (GDPR erasure on request).
- Search results within 800 ms p95.
- Nightly full backup; RPO 1 hour; RTO 4 hours.

## Constraints
- Team of 8. Java 21, Spring. PostgreSQL available. Existing ILS (Horizon) must be migrated in one shot.
- Kiosks run on Windows 10 tablets.
